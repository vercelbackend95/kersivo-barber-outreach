Inside the shop

The room

Walnut panels, brass mirrors and filament bulbs, in one long room with windows onto the street. Arrive early and there is a bench by the glass.

The chair

Black leather and chrome, with a headrest that tips back for the shave. You stay in the same chair from the first word to the final check in the mirror.

The tools

Clippers for the fade, scissors for the top, an open razor for the neckline, and a badger brush for the lather. Cleaned and laid out fresh for every customer.
