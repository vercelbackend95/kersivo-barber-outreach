## Regulatory basis

These internal records are designed around UK GDPR documentation obligations, including:

- **Controller documentation** of processing activities (Article 30(1) style)
- **Processor documentation** of processing carried out on behalf of each controller (Article 30(2) style)
- Recording of **recipients / providers** and **international transfers** where applicable
- Description of **security / technical and organisational measures (TOMs)** at an appropriate level
- **Retention** information where established; approved policy vs enforcement is distinguished in [retention-schedule.md](./retention-schedule.md). Gaps that remain open are exposed rather than invented

**ICO international-transfer terminology (2026):** the transfer risk assessment is also referred to as the **data protection test**. This ROPA and the companion transfer register use both labels where relevant. Completion of a TRA / data protection test is **not** inferred from a provider’s DPA alone.

## Dual-role framing (do not mix)

KERSIVO has **two** distinct data-protection roles:

1. **Independent controller** — for KERSIVO’s own account, authentication/security, SaaS billing, legal acceptance, support, tax/legal records, marketing analytics/ads (where consented), fraud/abuse prevention, KERSIVO operational audit records, and B2B prospecting/outreach (A15) (see Privacy Policy dual-role section).
2. **Processor** — for **Customer Personal Data** that a barbershop Client submits to, collects through, or manages using the Services, under the Data Processing Agreement at `/dpa` (version `2026-09-23`). See also the internal [retention schedule](./retention-schedule.md).

**PART A** and **PART B** below must be read separately. Do not treat marketing-controller processing as processor processing, or vice versa.
