## In short

There is no fixed rule. In some shops each chair renter runs their own booking account and pays for it; in others the shop runs one system and the cost is part of the chair rent. What matters is that it is agreed in writing, and that everyone knows who keeps the client list if a barber leaves.

## Two common set-ups

**1. Each renter has their own account.** The renter chooses the system, pays for it, takes their own payments and owns their client list. The shop has no shared diary, and clients see a different booking link for each barber.

**2. The shop runs one system for everyone.** One booking page and one link for the shop. The shop pays and either absorbs the cost or builds it into the chair rent. The renters' clients sit in the shop's account, so who can export what has to be agreed up front.

Many shops end up with a mix: a shared booking page, with each renter's payments going to their own account where the system allows it.

## How booking systems charge for extra barbers

Per-barber pricing decides how much the "one system for everyone" set-up costs. On published UK prices:

- **Booksy:** £40 a month for the first calendar plus £5 for each extra one, plus VAT. *(Checked 15 November 2026.)*
- **Fresha:** £14.95 a month for a one-person Independent plan; the Team plan is £9.95 a month per team member, plus VAT. *(Checked 15 November 2026.)*
- **Vagaro:** £10 a month for each additional bookable calendar. *(Checked 8 October 2026.)*
- **Timely:** unlimited team members on every plan; team size does not set the price. *(Checked 14 November 2026.)*
- **Setora:** no per-staff seat fees per location. *(Checked 8 October 2026.)*
- **KERSIVO:** Full KERSIVO is £39/month per location, not per barber. 0% KERSIVO commission; Stripe fees apply.

Per-barber pricing is the reason costs move apart as a shop grows: see [cost by team size](/compare#cost-by-team-size).

## Questions to agree in writing

- Who pays for the booking system, and does the chair rent change because of it?
- Whose account does each client sit in, and what happens to that client's details if the renter leaves?
- Who takes the payment for the haircut, and into whose account?
- Who sets prices and opening hours on the booking page?
- Who handles no-shows and deposits?
