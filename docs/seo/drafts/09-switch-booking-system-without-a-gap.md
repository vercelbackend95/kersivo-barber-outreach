## In short

Switching booking systems goes wrong in three places: the client list, appointments already booked in the old system, and the links clients use to book. Export first, run both systems side by side for a short overlap, move every link on one day, and only then close the old account.

## Before you change anything

- **Read your current contract.** Check the notice period, when the next billing date falls, and whether anything is paid annually.
- **Find out what you can export.** Most systems let the account owner export clients, sometimes appointments and sales. Check the format (usually CSV), who in the team is allowed to do it, and whether client marketing consent comes with it. Your platform's help pages describe its export. Where KERSIVO compares platforms, [who owns your clients](/compare#who-owns-your-clients) is set out side by side.
- **List every place your booking link lives:** Instagram bio, Google Business Profile, Facebook, your website, the card at the till, automatic messages.
- **Choose a quiet week.** Not December, not the week before a bank holiday.

## Step 1: Export everything

Export the client list (names, phone numbers, emails, notes and marketing consent where the system provides it) and, if you can, future appointments and service history. Save the files somewhere other than the old system. Do this before you give notice: some systems remove access when the account closes.

## Step 2: Set up the new system in full

Services, prices and times, each barber's hours, breaks, deposit and cancellation rules, and the confirmation and reminder messages. Import the client list. Make a test booking from your own phone, as a client would.

## Step 3: Overlap, don't jump

Keep the old system open for appointments already booked there and stop it taking new ones. If the old system lets you turn off online booking, do that. New bookings go into the new system from day one. Keep the overlap short: once the last appointment booked in the old system has passed, the overlap ends.

## Step 4: Move every link on the same day

Change the Instagram link, the Google Business Profile booking link, your website button and any printed QR codes on one day, so clients never land on the old system. Tell regulars directly: one short message with the new link is enough.

## Step 5: Close the old account

Once the last old appointment has passed and every link points at the new system, export once more (to catch anything added during the overlap), then close the account in writing.

## Common mistakes

- Closing the old account before exporting.
- Forgetting the Google Business Profile link, which often brings more bookings than Instagram.
- Changing prices and the booking system in the same week, so clients face two changes at once.
- Not checking marketing consent before sending messages to an imported list.
