> Live product is pure SaaS £39/month (immediate first payment, no pause, no automatic multi-location discount).
> Do not use deposit / go-live fee / “pause go-live fee” / first-month discount wording from this file as current commercial truth.
> Source of truth: `KERSIVO Commercial Offer v1.0.pdf` and `docs/gtm/offer-v1-compliance.md`.

## Offer name
`Kersivo Margin Recovery Sprint (30 days)`

## Target
- UK independent barbershops with 3-8 chairs.
- Owner-managed teams with active booking-app dependency.

## Core promise
- Migrate and launch with white-glove support.
- Track a pre-agreed KPI scorecard from day 1.
- Reduce perceived migration risk with staged commitment.

## Commercial structure
1. Sprint deposit: fixed onboarding fee.
2. Go-live fee: paid only when migration and core setup are complete.
3. Managed Care continuation: monthly service for hosting, admin, support, and incremental improvements.

## KPI outcome contract (example)
- Baseline period: previous 28 days.
- Validation period: first 28 live days.
- Primary KPI chosen at kickoff (one of):
  - utilizationRate,
  - noShowImpactGBP,
  - ownerOpsHoursWeekly.
- Secondary KPI: one supporting metric from the shared KPI set.

## Risk-reversal conditions
- If migration milestones are not delivered on agreed timeline by Kersivo, client can pause go-live fee until completion.
- If scorecard instrumentation is missing at go-live, first month of Managed Care is discounted.
- If client blocks required inputs for >7 days, timeline shifts but scorecard model remains.

## Delivery scope (sprint)
- Discovery + baseline metric capture.
- Booking migration and workflow mapping.
- Team setup, services, and operational checks.
- Domain + launch checklist.
- Staff handover session and post-launch review.

## Sales talk track (short)
- "This is not a generic website package. It is a guided migration sprint with measurable operating outcomes."
- "We agree KPI definitions before launch, then review before/after with your own data."
- "You get a staged commitment and clear risk controls instead of a blind platform switch."
