# Kersivo ICP: UK Margin-Leak Barbers (Primary Segment)

## Primary ICP (single segment)
- Location: UK, tier-1 and tier-2 cities with strong walk-in and repeat demand.
- Shop type: independent barbershop with 3-8 chairs.
- Team model: owner-managed, at least 2 active barbers with shared schedule pressure.
- Current stack: marketplace booking apps or mixed booking tools plus manual workarounds.
- Revenue posture: stable demand but visible margin pressure from platform fees, missed slots, and admin overhead.
- Decision maker: owner or partner who signs off on operations tools.

## Why this segment first
- Feels financial pain fast enough to switch.
- Small enough to decide quickly, large enough to feel process chaos.
- Has clear before/after economics that can be demonstrated in sales calls.
- Lower enterprise complexity than chains, higher urgency than solo chairs.

## Jobs-to-be-done (must-have framing)
1. Protect margin by reducing avoidable booking and workflow losses.
2. Keep calendar utilization high without adding front-desk chaos.
3. Run bookings, team diary, and pickup retail from one operating layer.
4. Keep customer relationship and brand traffic on owned channels.

## Qualifying criteria (must pass at least 4/6)
- Uses paid booking app and complains about fee drag.
- Reports recurring no-show or late-cancel slot waste.
- Owner spends >=4 hours/week on scheduling fixes or manual coordination.
- Wants tighter control of customer journey on own domain.
- Has at least one growth event in next 90 days (rebrand, hiring, expansion, relocation).
- Ready to make process changes in <=30 days.

## Exclusion criteria (do not target now)
- Solo chair with low booking volume and no operational pain.
- Chain franchise with centralized procurement and long vendor cycles.
- Shops currently rebuilding full brand/website with another agency.
- Owners explicitly unwilling to migrate any operational flow.

## Sales hypothesis for this ICP
If Kersivo is positioned as a margin-protection and utilization-improvement system (not a generic all-in-one),
then owner-managed 3-8 chair shops will engage at higher rates because the value maps to immediate business pain.

## Messaging spine (for outreach and landing)
- Headline promise: "Stop margin leaks and recover wasted slots."
- Proof language: "Measured before/after on utilization, no-show impact, and owner admin time."
- Risk framing: "White-glove migration and go-live support, not DIY setup."
